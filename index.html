What is AI?

Discover what Artificial Intelligence is and how it works.

Artificial Intelligence is when computers do tasks that usually need human thinking, like understanding speech, recognising pictures or answering questions. AI does not really "think" like a person: it finds patterns in huge amounts of examples and uses them to make its best guess.

📊
02

Machine Learning

Learn how machines learn from data and improve.

Machine learning is how most AI is built. Instead of writing every rule by hand, we show the computer thousands of examples, such as photos labelled "cat" or "dog". It slowly adjusts itself until its answers are mostly right, and the more good examples it sees, the better it gets.

📱
03

AI in Everyday Life

See how AI is already part of your daily life.

You probably use AI every day: face unlock on your phone, video and music recommendations, map apps that predict traffic, spam filters in your email, and translators. Chatbots that answer questions and help with ideas are AI too.

⚖️
04

AI Ethics

Explore the challenges and responsibilities of AI.

AI can make mistakes and can copy unfair ideas hidden in the data it learned from. That is why it matters to check important facts, protect your personal information, and be honest about when you used AI in your work. People, not machines, stay responsible for decisions.
