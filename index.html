About AI Compass

AI Compass is your starting point for exploring the world of Artificial Intelligence. It was made by students as a CAS project, to help anyone understand how AI works, how to use it wisely, and how to think about it critically.

    Free for everyone

    No sign-up, no ads, no cost. Just open it and learn.
