    What these numbers mean

    • They count devices, not people. A phone and a laptop of one person are two devices; one school computer shared by a class is one.
    • Each device counts once per day, however many pages it opens. Days run from midnight to midnight UTC.
    • No personal data is kept. A device is only a random number made by its browser: no names, no e-mail addresses, no IP addresses. After 13 months, or when a browser's data is cleared, the same device counts as new.
    • Not everyone is counted: browsers with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control switched on, test robots, and the site opened on your own computer (localhost) while you work on it.
    • A day counts at most 20,000 devices, and at most 2,000 of them new. Anyone could send made-up visits, so these limits keep the free database from filling up. If the numbers ever stop at them, the site has grown: raise the two limits in hello() in supabase/schema.sql (part 8).
    • The numbers start on the day counting was switched on. Nothing before that day is known. The daily list behind them is deleted after 35 days; the totals of each day stay.
